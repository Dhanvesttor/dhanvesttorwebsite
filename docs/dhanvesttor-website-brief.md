**Positioning.** Designed by women, for women. The site should *feel* it (imagery,
tone, the founder's story, CLCB) without saying "women only" on every page. A man
landing on the PMS page should see a serious fund house and feel welcome.

**Brand feel.** Premium but approachable. Waterfield is the benchmark for premium
(editorial serif, full-bleed photography, calm whitespace) but it intimidates.
Dhanvesttor sits one notch warmer: the confidence of a fund house, the openness of
an expert friend. Real photography, generous space, one idea per screen, smooth
motion, no infographic walls, no AI-template look.

**Audience, in order.** 1) Prospective PMS clients (HNI/UHNI women and families,
₹50 L+). 2) Prospective MF investors (₹5,000+ SIP, often CLCB alumni).
3) Women curious about finance who join CLCB first. 4) Existing clients (login).
5) Candidates and press.

**Funnel the site must mirror** (from the sales programme): CLCB is the entry
point, mutual funds build the relationship, PMS is the conversion. Insurance and
bonds complete the picture for existing clients.

**Success.** Qualified PMS and MF enquiries, CLCB registrations, organic rankings
("PMS Kolkata", "women wealth management India", "SIP calculator", "PMS vs mutual
fund"), and a blog the marketing team publishes to weekly without a developer.

**Anti-goals.** Finance clichés (handshakes, arrows, coins), dense infographics,
carousels, generic gradients, jargon without a gloss, any return figure in a hero,
anything that makes a first-time investor feel small.

## 3. Brand direction ✎ (see the board)

Built from the logo you are keeping: raspberry `#DE4D7A` + marigold `#F8C233`
mark, indigo `#372F83` brochure headings, terracotta `#C8876A` tagline.

- **Palette.** Ivory `#FBF7F0` (70% of every screen) · Indigo `#2E2A6E`
  (headlines, buttons, dark sections) · Raspberry `#DE4D7A` (one accent per
  screen, links, eyebrows) · Marigold `#F8C233` (hairlines only) · Terracotta
  `#C8876A` (CLCB, warm editorial) · Sand `#E9DCC7` (alternate backgrounds).
  The orange buttons and cream-yellow background of the current site go.
- **Type.** Fraunces (light, large, optical size 144) for headlines; Hanken
  Grotesk for body and UI. Both free Google Fonts.
- **Logo.** Unchanged. Centred in the header on desktop, left on mobile with
  login right.
- **Imagery.** Real photography: daylight, Kolkata interiors, conversation,
  hands and faces. Placeholders (Unsplash) during build; your CLCB photographs
  and a founder/team shoot replace them. Shoot brief included in §7.
- **Motion.** 400ms page transitions, one reveal per section, 2px button lift,
  count-up numbers, reduced-motion respected.
- **Shape motif.** The logo's quarter-circle and arrow: one-rounded-corner image
  mattes, a 55/45 raspberry-marigold hairline, Fraunces section numerals.
- **Voice.** Second person, short sentences, explain before sell, gloss every
  term. "Her Money, Her Growth" is the signature line in footer, CLCB and About.

## 5. Build ✎

- **Frontend.** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + Framer
  Motion. Server-rendered with incremental revalidation so new posts appear
  without a redeploy.
- **CMS.** Headless **WordPress** (your existing install) via WPGraphQL ✎:
  marketing keeps the editor it knows; HR gets an Editor role limited to the
  Careers post type; events, team, press and FAQs become custom post types with
  SEO fields (Yoast/RankMath carried through). Alternative: Sanity, if you
  prefer a cleaner studio and no WordPress maintenance.
- **Hosting.** Hostinger, as asked. The Next.js app needs Node: Hostinger VPS or
  a Node-capable plan ⚠ which plan do you have? If it is PHP-only shared
  hosting, we ship a static export rebuilt automatically on each publish.
- **SEO.** Per-page metadata, Open Graph, JSON-LD (Organization,
  FinancialService, Article, FAQ, Breadcrumb, Person for team), sitemap,
  robots, canonicals, image alt discipline, Core Web Vitals budget, keyword-led
  blog taxonomy, calculator pages targeting long-tail queries.
- **Forms.** Enquiry (PMS / MF / CLCB / Insurance / Bonds), CLCB registration,
  careers with CV upload, newsletter. Delivered by email and stored in WordPress.
  WhatsApp CTA retained.
- **Logins.** Header button → login.dhanvesttor.com; PMS Login → Kotak FA
  Connect. No auth on the site itself.
- **Accessibility.** WCAG 2.1 AA, keyboard, reduced-motion, 16px side gutters.
