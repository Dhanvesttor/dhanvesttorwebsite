Kolkata, April 2026. Dhanvesttor, the SEBI-registered portfolio manager and women-first wealth firm, has announced plans to expand its operations into North and West India over the next 12 to 18 months.

The expansion will carry both sides of the firm's work: portfolio management and mutual fund distribution for clients, and the Co-Learning and Community Building (CLCB) programme that brings financial education to women who may never become paying clients.

"Our ambition is to reach more than two lakh women with financial education," said founder and CEO Anooshka Soham Bathwal. "For one woman that means managing an investment portfolio. For another it means understanding her savings, asking the right question, or making her first independent financial decision. Both are greater control over her future."

Dhanvesttor was incorporated in 2023 and received its SEBI portfolio manager registration in February 2024. Its flagship FAITH Fund is a flexi-cap strategy managed by Ishan Daga. The firm distributes mutual funds from more than twenty fund houses and runs CLCB workshops, learning circles and community events across Bengal.
